# OWASP LLM Top 10 (2025): Unit B checkpoint

Unit B (call intelligence) takes a recorded sales call, transcribes it and returns stage 1 (the transcript) to the CRM through a signed callback. This note checks Unit B against each item of the **2025** OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications. For each item it records whether the item applies, the control, the file that holds it, and either the test that proves it or the batch that will build it.

**State at this checkpoint (branch `call-intelligence`, Unit B batch 3: wave 2 and stage 2 delivery).** No real speech-to-text adapter exists yet. `build_transcriber` refuses to build one, and the only transcriber is `FakeTranscriber`. **Call content reaches a language model in two waves.** Wave 1 runs `unit_b.extract` and `unit_b.prose` (`units/call_intelligence/passes.py`) on every transcribed call, and stage 1 carries their analysis. Wave 2 runs on an eligible call once call.stage1 has gone (`units/call_intelligence/wave2.py`, on its own queue and worker). It has five passes, `unit_b.objections`, `unit_b.score`, `unit_b.escalations`, `unit_b.coaching` and `unit_b.extras`, and call.stage2 carries their parts. Signals, number detection and alarm phrases run in code with no model. Voice ID, voice qualities (prosody), translation and re-analysis are not built. Where a control belongs to a pass that has not been built, the paragraph says so and names the batch that owes it. "Not built" means exactly that; it is not a claim that the risk is absent.

Scope: `src/dodeal_ai/units/call_intelligence/`, `src/dodeal_ai/api/routes/calls.py`, `src/dodeal_ai/workers/calls.py`, and the Unit B parts of core: `core/jobs.py`, `core/audio_download.py`, `core/callbacks.py`, `core/cost/limiter.py` (the calls budgets) and `core/cost/spend.py`.

## LLM01: Prompt injection

**Applies now.** A caller controls what is said on a call, so every word of a transcript is attacker-shaped text in the same way a note is for Unit A. Wave 1 puts the transcript into two prompts, and the controls are these:

- **Delimited, and first.** The transcript goes only in the `variable` half, between the fixed BEGIN/END CALLER DATA markers, and renders *before* the instructions (`core/prompting.py`, `AssembledPrompt.data_first`). The instructions come only from versioned files under `prompts/call_intelligence/`, stamped `unit_b_prompts_v18`, with their digest and each file's hash pinned beside the stamp.
- **Neutralised.** `build_prompt` defuses a forged BEGIN or END marker spoken into a segment, as it does for a note. Each segment is one line, `[s<n> mm:ss agent|client] text`, with its whitespace folded (`units/call_intelligence/prompts.py::render_transcript`), so no segment can start a line of its own and pass for another segment or for the LANGUAGE line.
- **Never a rejected answer.** The reprompt adds only the tail file; the prose pass reads the *settled* extraction, which has already been validated and quote-checked, inside the data half.

- **Batch 4's passes, the same way.** `roles_v1` shows the engine's `speaker_N` labels, `extras_v2` the company's VOCABULARY list and `translate_v1` a TARGET line. All three go inside the data half, and each is a new file: no stamped file was edited.

**Proved by** `tests/unit/test_unit_b_prompts.py::test_a_delimiter_spoken_on_the_call_is_neutralised`, `::test_a_segment_cannot_forge_a_line_of_its_own`, `::test_the_transcript_comes_first_and_the_instructions_after`, `tests/unit/test_unit_b_prompt_set_stamp.py::test_the_unit_b_prompt_set_digest_matches_its_stamp` and `tests/unit/test_call_passes.py::test_an_invented_quote_reprompts_then_fails_the_pass` (the second prompt differs from the first in its tail alone). The shared machinery is also proved by `tests/security/test_unit_a_injection.py::test_a_forged_delimiter_is_neutralised_in_every_prompt`.

## LLM02: Sensitive information disclosure

**Applies now.** A call carries names, phone numbers and prices, and the push carries a signed link, two phone hashes and possibly a voiceprint.

- **Nothing reaches a log.** The audio link, the hashes, the voiceprint and every word of the transcript stay out of every log line, repr and exception. `Segment.text` and `Transcript.segments` are `repr=False` (`transcriber.py`), `Job.metadata` is `repr=False` (`core/jobs.py`), and download and callback errors carry fixed reason codes, unchained. **Proved by** `test_calls_route.py::test_no_line_carries_the_link_a_hash_or_the_voiceprint`, `test_call_observability.py::test_the_outcome_line_is_complete_and_carries_no_content`, `test_calls_read_route.py::test_every_read_is_one_audit_line_with_no_content`, `test_transcriber_conformance.py::test_segment_text_is_never_on_a_repr` and `test_audio_download.py::test_a_transport_failure_is_retryable_and_carries_no_url`.
- **A 72-hour hold.** `result_ttl_seconds` defaults to 259200 and cannot be raised above it (`config.py`). A finished job and its dedupe index expire on the same TTL (`core/jobs.py`), and the recording itself is deleted in a `finally` (`core/audio_download.py`). **Proved by** `test_calls_config.py::test_anything_else_malformed_is_refused` (259201 refused), `test_jobs.py::test_a_terminal_transition_is_final_and_expires_job_and_index` and `test_audio_download.py::test_the_file_is_deleted_when_the_callers_block_raises`.
- **Tenant isolation.** Every key carries the tenant. **Proved by** `test_jobs.py::test_the_same_call_id_in_two_tenants_is_two_jobs` and `test_calls_read_route.py::test_another_tenants_job_is_404`.
- **Numbers masked before any model, prices kept.** The passes read a *prompt copy* of each segment (`units/call_intelligence/numbers.py::prompt_copy`): every phone number, written in Western or Arabic-Indic digits or spoken in English or Arabic (dialect forms included), becomes `[PHONE]`, and every email address becomes `[EMAIL]`. A price such as "1,200,000 AED" is not phone-shaped and is kept, because the extraction needs it. The stored transcript keeps what was said. The copy is masked whether or not the tenant's `number_detection_enabled` switch is on; the switch gates only the finds and escalations. A find carries the last four digits and what the number matched, never the number or its hash, and a quote is checked against the masked copy, so no quote can carry a number back out. **Proved by** `tests/unit/test_call_numbers.py::test_a_price_is_not_masked`, `::test_the_prompt_copy_masks_phones_and_emails_only`, `::test_a_spoken_number_is_masked_whole`, `tests/unit/test_call_stage1.py::test_the_model_never_reads_the_agents_number` and `tests/unit/test_call_passes.py::test_a_masked_number_may_be_quoted_as_masked`.
- **Any long digit run is masked, not only a phone number.** The prompt copy also masks every run of 9 to 15 digits joined only by spaces, full stops or hyphens, whatever it starts with. That covers another country's number said without its +, an ID and an account number. A price grouped in thousands with commas ("1,200,000", the Arabic separator too) is kept whole and never joined to the digits beside it. This wider mask changes what a model reads, never the finds: a run masked this way is not a find and raises no escalation. Every wave 1 and wave 2 pass reads the same copy (`evidence.CallText`). **Proved by** `tests/unit/test_call_numbers.py::test_a_long_digit_run_is_masked_but_a_price_is_not` ("966 50 123 4567" masked, "1,200,000" kept), `::test_the_prompt_copy_masks_every_run_of_nine_to_fifteen_digits` and `::test_a_masked_run_is_not_a_find`.
- **Audio goes to the speech-to-text vendor, and only there.** Gemini gets a file inline under 12 MiB. A larger one goes through the Files API and is deleted in a `finally`; a failed delete is logged by type only, and Google deletes files after 48 hours anyway. An HTTP engine gets one multipart POST. Nothing else leaves: no keyword list and no custom vocabulary is sent. **Proved by** `tests/unit/test_gemini_transcriber.py::test_the_upload_is_deleted_even_when_the_transcription_fails`, `::test_a_failed_delete_is_logged_by_type_and_the_call_still_succeeds` and `::test_spk_1_and_spk_2_come_back_as_ordered_speaker_segments` (the request body).
- **A card number is masked too.** The digit-run mask now covers 9 to 19 digits. **Proved by** `tests/unit/test_call_numbers.py::test_the_prompt_copy_masks_every_run_of_nine_to_nineteen_digits` (`sixteen-card`).
- **Reasoning text is never stored, logged or delivered.** A reasoning model's thinking never crosses the seam. The adapter reads only `message.content` (`core/llm/openai_compatible.py::_parse`), and `LLMResponse` has no field that could hold reasoning text. Only its token count comes through, as `reasoning_tokens`, and that count goes on the stage-2 outcome line. Every pass's answer schema is `extra="forbid"`, so an answer that carries its own reasoning is malformed and never becomes a part. **Proved by** `tests/unit/test_openai_compatible_adapter.py::test_a_reasoning_models_thoughts_never_cross_the_seam`, `tests/unit/test_call_ceilings.py::test_no_reasoning_reaches_a_stage2_part` and `::test_an_answer_carrying_its_reasoning_is_refused`.

## LLM03: Supply chain

**Applies now, and grows with the first adapter.** The supply here is:

- **Speech-to-text vendors.** There are three adapters: `gemini.py` (Google's `google-genai` SDK, pinned at `2.25.0`, on the Interactions API), `http_stt.py::OpenAiCompatibleTranscriber` and `http_stt.py::DiarizedHttpTranscriber`. All three pass the conformance suite on recorded responses. The fake is demo-only and is never built from configuration.
- **The SDK's hidden retries are off.** The Interactions client's retry is switched off through `sdk_configuration.retry_config`, because its public options never go below one retry. The Files client gets one attempt. A pinned SDK upgrade that moved either switch fails the test. **Proved by** `test_gemini_transcriber.py::test_a_503_or_a_429_makes_exactly_one_request_on_either_layer`, `test_transcriber_conformance.py` and `test_http_stt.py::test_a_tenants_stt_profile_selects_its_adapter`.
- **Providers and models are pinned by configuration and stamped.** `DODEAL_CALL_STT_MODEL`, the STT profiles, `DODEAL_LLM_PROVIDERS` and `DODEAL_MODEL_ROUTES` are all set by configuration. Every pass's provider and model is stamped under `versions.passes`.
- **Open models that may run on the device for voice ID and prosody.** None are present. Their passes are owed by later batches, which must pin model files by version and hash.
- **Pinned versions.** Every Python dependency is locked in `uv.lock`. The model ID is pinned by configuration. The provider and model a transcript actually came from are recorded on it and on every job outcome line (`test_call_observability.py::test_the_outcome_line_is_complete_and_carries_no_content`).

**Owed by a person:** the vendor's data-processing terms, and the region where audio is stored (ASSUMPTIONS 3.6, B13).

## LLM04: Data and model poisoning

**Does not apply.** Nothing in Unit B trains, fine-tunes, or builds an index from call audio, transcripts or voiceprints. Every model is used through a request/response interface that keeps no state between calls. This becomes live the day any tenant's calls are used to tune or index anything.

## LLM05: Improper output handling

**Applies now, to the transcript and to model output.** A transcriber's output is untrusted until it is checked. `Transcript` validates itself on construction (`transcriber.py`): segments must be in order and never overlap, the language profile and the uncertainty flag are recomputed in code, and `eligible_for_full_analysis` is decided in code (`worker.py::stage1_result`). **Proved by** `test_transcriber_conformance.py::test_a_transcript_that_contradicts_its_segments_is_refused`, `::test_segments_are_ordered_and_never_overlap` and `test_process_call.py::test_short_or_uncertain_calls_are_not_eligible_for_full_analysis`.

Both passes' answers go through `llm_call.parse_output` against `extra="forbid"` schemas (`passes.Extraction`, `passes.Prose`) before anything believes them. Then come the rules a schema cannot hold, all in code (`passes.py`):

- **The quote check, and its matching rule** (`evidence.py`). The prompts ask for a quote of at most 15 words; the check allows 40. The segment it cites must exist, and the quote's words, normalised as the alarm matcher normalises (`alarms.py::normalise`), must appear in that segment, as the model read it, in the same order. Between two of them the segment may hold only a word that repeats the word before it ("عمري عمري"), or a filler on the fixed, versioned list `quote_fillers_v1` (Arabic and English: يعني، اه، ايوه، um, uh and the like). **A negation is never a filler** (ما، مش، لا، لم، لن، مو، ليس, not, no, never, don't and the like): "انا عمري زرت دبي" does not match "انا عمري ما زرت دبي", so a quote can never drop the word that reverses it. A quote not in the segment it cites is looked for in the segment just before, then just after; found there, that segment is stored, and a speaker is read there.
- **Field by field in the extraction.** A detail whose quote fails is kept `uncertain`, its quote and segment null and `evidence_failed: true`; an element (wanted, concerns, agreed, next_step, loss_reason) is kept with `unverified: true` and no quote; the mood alike. The answer is malformed -- one reprompt, then stage 1 goes with analysis null and its `analysis_reason` -- only on a broken shape or when more than half of its quotes fail. A failed quote never reaches the prose pass. The extras are kept field by field the same way: a keyword whose quote fails is dropped, a seriousness check or the agent dialect kept unverified with no quote (an unverified yes left out of the band); a keyword longer than five words is dropped, a canonical name off the list becomes null, and a WhatsApp text too long or in the wrong script is asked for once more with a fixed tail, then delivered null with its reason. Every other wave-2 pass, the score first, stays strict: any failing quote is malformed.
- A detail marked `not_mentioned` carries no value, quote or segment, and one marked `stated` carries a value.
- A next step's time is resolved by the model but held in code: refused before the call or more than 90 days after it, `booked` only with a kept time on a verified quote.
- The CRM note is at most 80 words, and the summary and note are in the language decided in code (`language.py`), never by the model.

**Proved by** `tests/unit/test_call_quote_match.py::test_a_repeated_word_in_the_segment_may_be_left_out_of_the_quote`, `::test_a_negation_left_out_of_the_quote_fails`, `::test_one_changed_word_fails`, `::test_a_quote_found_in_s12_while_citing_s11_is_stored_as_s12`, `tests/unit/test_call_passes.py::test_one_bad_detail_quote_keeps_the_pass_and_that_detail_uncertain`, `::test_six_of_ten_quotes_failing_is_malformed`, `::test_every_broken_detail_quote_is_that_details_evidence_failed`, `::test_a_mood_quote_is_checked_too`, `tests/unit/test_call_next_step.py::test_a_time_before_the_call_or_past_90_days_is_refused`, `tests/unit/test_call_score.py::test_one_failing_quote_among_true_ones_still_leaves_no_score`, `::test_a_long_note_or_the_wrong_language_is_malformed`, `tests/unit/test_call_language.py::test_the_summary_language_follows_the_table` and `tests/unit/test_call_stage1.py::test_a_malformed_extraction_twice_fails_the_pass_without_the_prose`.

**The quote check runs on every pass and on every summary element.** In wave 1 (`extract_v2`), what the client wanted, every concern, every agreement and the next step's action each carry a quote and a segment, and each is checked. In wave 2 every pass is checked the same way (`units/call_intelligence/evidence.py`):

- objections: the client's quote from a client segment, the agent's answer from an agent segment;
- the score's checks: a quote wherever the answer says something was said;
- escalations: each flag's quote, from the agent for an agent's issue;
- coaching: every observation and every stage marked done;
- extras: every keyword's words in the segment it cites, and every seriousness check answered yes.

A quote given where none is owed is still checked. Who said a thing and when are read from the cited segment in code, never from the model. Every number a stage-2 part carries is computed in code from the checked answers: the score's marks, total and band (`score.py`), the seriousness band (`extras.py`) and the moments' timestamps (`coaching.py`). A pass that fails keeps its part null with a reason, and the other parts still go (`wave2.py`). **Proved by** `tests/unit/test_call_passes.py::test_every_elements_evidence_is_quote_checked`, `::test_an_agreed_item_without_a_real_quote_is_kept_unverified`, `tests/unit/test_call_objections.py::test_every_quote_is_checked_and_from_its_speaker`, `::test_a_quote_given_where_none_is_owed_is_still_checked`, `tests/unit/test_call_score.py::test_a_quote_is_owed_where_the_answer_says_something_was_said`, `tests/unit/test_call_escalations.py::test_every_flag_is_quote_checked_and_an_agent_issue_is_the_agents`, `tests/unit/test_call_coaching.py::test_every_quote_is_checked`, `tests/unit/test_call_extras.py::test_a_keyword_not_said_in_its_segment_is_malformed`, `::test_every_seriousness_quote_is_checked_and_a_yes_is_owed_one`, `::test_the_band_is_computed_in_code_from_the_yes_count` and `tests/unit/test_call_stage2.py::test_one_failed_pass_still_delivers_the_others`.

**Batch 4's outputs, checked in code the same way:**

- **roles.** Each agent or client is quoted from that voice's own segment. The mapping is applied only when exactly one voice is the agent and none is unclear; otherwise the transcript is uncertain.
- **re-analysis.** The pushed transcript is validated as a `Transcript`. The job never transcribes, and a job with no transcript fails unpaid.
- **translation.** Each chunk's segment ids must come back once each, in order, in the target script. Ids, times and speakers are the transcript's, never the model's.
- **extras.** A keyword's `canonical` must be a name on the company's list, and its `said` a name of at most five words.
- **possible_broker.** A flag rests on the client's own words, from a client segment; one from the agent's is malformed.
- **loss_reason.** A category rests on the client's own quote. The coaching's `ask_why` tip is fixed text in code, never a model's.

**Proved by** `tests/unit/test_call_roles.py::test_an_invented_or_borrowed_quote_fails_the_check`, `tests/unit/test_call_extras.py::test_a_seven_word_said_is_rejected_and_five_are_a_name`, `tests/unit/test_call_possible_broker.py::test_possible_broker_rests_on_the_clients_own_words`, `tests/unit/test_call_loss_reason.py::test_a_reason_is_the_clients_own_quote_or_kept_unverified`, `tests/unit/test_call_reanalysis.py::test_a_reanalysis_never_calls_the_transcriber`, `tests/unit/test_call_translation.py::test_segment_ids_speakers_and_times_survive_the_translation` and `tests/unit/test_call_keywords.py::test_the_extras_result_uses_the_listed_canonical_name`.

## LLM06: Excessive agency

**Applies, and is held by design.** The service sends nothing to anyone except one signed POST, to the callback URL the tenant configured (`core/callbacks.py`). There is no messaging API, no CRM write and no tool call, so a model can trigger no action at all. A URL in a push body can never become a destination: the body is `extra="forbid"`, and `audio_url` is only ever fetched, never posted to. **Proved by** `test_callbacks.py::test_stage1_goes_signed_to_the_callback_url_only` and `test_calls_route.py::test_a_malformed_body_is_422_and_admits_nothing` (a `callback_url` in the body is a 422).

**The WhatsApp suggestion is never sent by this service.** `unit_b.extras` writes a follow-up of at most 60 words in the summary language. It goes back to the CRM as text inside the call.stage2 body (`extras_part`, `whatsapp_suggestion`) and nowhere else. The agent decides whether to send it, from the CRM. The service's only outbound POSTs are the signed callback and the model call. The extras module imports no HTTP client and nothing that queues or sends. The prompt tells the model the text is a suggestion to review, and that it must carry no phone number, email address or link. Stage 2's own events, call.stage2 and call.failed for stage 2, leave through the same signed POST to the tenant's URL. **Proved by** `tests/unit/test_call_extras.py::test_the_whatsapp_suggestion_has_no_way_out_but_the_callback`, `::test_a_whatsapp_suggestion_over_60_words_is_malformed` and `tests/unit/test_callbacks.py::test_stage2_goes_signed_as_call_stage2_on_its_own_delivery`.

## LLM07: System prompt leakage

**Applies now.** Unit B has ten templates, all instructions only, pinned by hash. The rule, already held for Unit A, is that a prompt carries no secret. Keys are read in one place each and never enter a prompt:

- the LLM key, in the adapter's header builder;
- a registry provider's key, in `core/llm/routing.py::_provider_key`;
- an STT key, in `units/call_intelligence/stt.py::_api_key`;
- the callback secret, in `core/callbacks.py::_signature`. **Proved by** `test_callbacks.py::test_the_callback_secrets_are_read_in_one_place` and, for the LLM key, `tests/unit/test_openai_compatible_adapter.py::test_key_is_never_in_the_body_or_the_url`. **Owed by the analysis batch:** a scan asserting that no Unit B template contains a key, a tenant name or a URL.

## LLM08: Vector and embedding weaknesses

**Applies to the voiceprint.** This service keeps no vector store. The only embedding is `agent_voiceprint`, which the CRM holds and sends with a push. Its schema is strict base64, capped at 16384 characters (`schemas.py`). It is kept with the job only while the tenant's `voice_id_enabled` consent switch is on, and dropped otherwise (`admission.py::job_metadata`). It is never logged, never put in a callback body, and expires with the job. **Proved by** `test_calls_route.py::test_the_voiceprint_is_kept_only_under_the_voice_id_switch`, `::test_no_line_carries_the_link_a_hash_or_the_voiceprint` and `::test_a_malformed_body_is_422_and_admits_nothing` (non-base64 and oversize voiceprints). **Owed by the voice ID batch:** the comparison itself, run on the device, and a test that a voiceprint never leaves it.

## LLM09: Misinformation

**Applies.** The rule is never to invent. A call with nothing to transcribe gets an outcome label (voicemail, no answer, too short) and a null transcript, never a made-up one (`worker.py::unpaid_outcome`). A transcript whose time-weighted confidence is below 0.6, or that holds no speech, is flagged uncertain in code (`transcriber.py::is_uncertain`). **Proved by** `test_process_call.py::test_a_short_or_unanswered_call_is_done_with_its_label_unpaid`, `test_transcriber_conformance.py::test_low_confidence_is_uncertain_by_spoken_time` and `::test_no_speech_is_uncertain_and_other`.

Wave 1 keeps to the same rule:

- **`not_mentioned` stays null.** The extraction prompt tells the model never to fill a detail from general knowledge. A detail marked not mentioned must carry no value, and one that carries a value anyway is malformed, not repaired. The prose prompt never writes a not-mentioned detail as known.
- **`uncertain` is decided in code.** A stated detail that cites a segment below the confidence floor is marked uncertain (`passes.py::settled`). An uncertain transcript still gets wave 1, but with `analysis.uncertain` true and every detail and the mood marked uncertain. The prose pass is told to write an uncertain detail as uncertain.
- **Failure is visible.** A pass that fails leaves analysis null with `analysis_reason`; it never ships a half-built analysis.

**Proved by** `tests/unit/test_call_passes.py::test_a_not_mentioned_budget_stays_null`, `::test_a_not_mentioned_detail_with_a_value_is_malformed`, `::test_a_detail_citing_a_low_confidence_segment_is_uncertain`, `::test_an_uncertain_transcript_makes_every_field_uncertain`, `tests/unit/test_call_stage1.py::test_an_uncertain_transcript_gets_wave_1_with_every_field_uncertain` and `::test_a_model_outage_delivers_the_transcript_with_analysis_null`.

Wave 2 never states as fact what it cannot check:

- **Claims to verify.** A model may hear a wrong price or wrong terms, but nothing here can tell whether they were wrong: there is no project feed. So a `wrong_price_or_terms` flag goes out typed `claim_to_verify`, never as a finding (`escalations.py`).
- **Components suppressed, not guessed.** The score's objections component is suppressed when no objection was raised. Its product-knowledge component is suppressed when the client asked no product question, and is marked `correctness_unverified` when it is marked at all. A suppressed component leaves the applicable weight instead of scoring zero (`score.py`).
- **No score at all where one would mislead.** With scoring off, an ineligible call, a client who barely spoke or no objections answer, the score is null with its reason.

**Proved by** `tests/unit/test_call_escalations.py::test_stage1s_and_the_models_escalations_merge_by_time` (a price claim goes out as `claim_to_verify`), `::test_an_issue_off_the_list_is_refused_by_the_schema`, `tests/unit/test_call_score.py::test_no_objection_and_no_product_question_are_suppressed_and_left_out`, `::test_product_knowledge` and `::test_each_null_reason`.

## LLM10: Unbounded consumption

**Applies now.**

- **Budgets, each on its own keys and never on the live pair.** `cost:calls:tenant` caps pushes (2000 a day), `tokens:calls:tenant` caps analysis tokens, and `audio_seconds:calls:tenant` caps transcription (360000 seconds a day, charged at download).
- **Caps.** The download is at most `max_audio_bytes` (with a 200 MiB ceiling) and must finish inside `CALL_DOWNLOAD_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`. Each job gets at most `CALL_MAX_TRIES` attempts and one job per call. A transcription is retried **once**, and only when its response never arrived (the lead's override of the never-retry rule, BRD B6). The second loss dead-letters the job. A stored transcript is never paid for again.
- **Every paid pass is kept, counted and started at most twice** (`paid.py`), in both waves. A received answer is never paid for again. A malformed answer gets its one reprompt, then the pass fails and only its part is null.
- **Output ceilings follow the profile.** Each wave 2 pass declares two ceilings: objections, score and escalations 2500 and 6000; coaching and extras 2000 and 4000. It takes the larger only when its profile sets a `reasoning_effort` (`core/llm/profiles.py::task_ceiling`). A profile may lower either ceiling, never raise it.
- **Stage 2 is bounded like stage 1.** It checks the calls budgets before any spend, stops itself before arq's timeout, and fails after `STAGE2_TRIES` runs. A stage 2 whose run was lost is re-queued once by the sweep and then failed with `stage2_lost`, so it is never pending forever. The sweep reads running jobs first, so a queued backlog never delays a stuck one.
- **Fails closed.** A worker pauses (`paused_budget`) over a budget *or* with the budget store down, and the job store refuses rather than guessing. Every task's spend is priced on its outcome line, and the stage-2 line carries each pass's tokens and reasoning tokens.
