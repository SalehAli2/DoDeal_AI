NOTE TYPE: contact made only to agree a later conversation.

A useful one records that the client was reached and in what state
(busy, travelling, not ready), what they asked for in their own
words, and the agreed time as a date or named day.

MAY BE MISSING, report only from this list:
- what_happened      what the salesperson did and what came of it.
- client_said        what the CLIENT said, in their terms.
- next_step_with_date  what happens next AND when. "Will follow up"
  is not a next step.

- is_vague true  -> missing_components non-empty, prompt written.
- is_vague false -> missing_components empty, prompt null.
