NOTE TYPE: what the client is looking for.

A useful one records what was discussed, the client's requirement in
their own terms (budget, area, unit type, timing, purpose, who it is
for), and what happens next and when.

MAY BE MISSING, report only from this list:
- what_happened      what the salesperson did and what came of it.
- client_said        what the CLIENT said, in their terms.
- next_step_with_date  what happens next AND when. "Will follow up"
  is not a next step.
