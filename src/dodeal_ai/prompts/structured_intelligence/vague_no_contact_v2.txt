NOTE TYPE: an attempt to reach the client that did not succeed.

A useful one records how they were approached, what happened (no
answer, busy, phone off, wrong number, message left), and when the
next attempt will be.

MAY BE MISSING, report only from this list:
- next_step_with_date   what happens next AND when, as a date or a
  named day. "Will follow up" is not a next step. A relative time
  counts: "tomorrow", "after 2 hrs", "next Tuesday".

Nothing else may be reported for this type.
